Common document checklist

  • Proof of present address, as accepted for the application category.
  • Proof of date of birth, such as an accepted birth certificate or other listed document.
  • Proof of identity and nationality, as applicable to the applicant.
  • For reissue: existing passport and copies of relevant pages.
  • Original documents and self-attested photocopies, as required by the official checklist.
  • Application reference and appointment details.

Requirements depend on age, application type, and circumstances. Use Passport Seva’s Document Advisor for the applicant-specific list.

Photo guidance

For adults attending a Passport Seva Kendra or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra, a separate photograph is generally not required because the photo is captured at the centre. For children under 4, a recent 4.5 × 3.5 cm colour photo with a white background is required. Photo rules can differ at other application collection centres—confirm current official instructions before printing photos.

02 OUR GLOBAL CONNECTION

A wider world,
closer at hand.

Uniglobe Travel International began in Vancouver, British Columbia, in 1981, founded by entrepreneur U. Gary Charlwood. It is described as the world’s largest single-brand travel franchise organization, with hundreds of agencies across 60+ countries on six continents. Public sources report several billion US dollars in annual system-wide sales; published figures vary.

The network works across small and mid-sized business travel management as well as leisure travel. It brings together local agencies with international reach. Independent agencies may join through programs such as Global Partner, while retaining their own local identity and independence.

Shree Aakash Travel Services is proudly connected with the Uniglobe Travel network.

Common requirements to check

  • Original passport with validity required by the destination (six months is common, but rules vary).
  • Completed application form and appointment or submission confirmation, if required.
  • Recent photographs in the exact size, background, and format specified for that visa. Some checklists request, for example, four 35 × 45 mm colour photos, a white background, matte finish, and specified face coverage; confirm the destination’s current rules.
  • Travel itinerary and accommodation details, where requested.
  • Recent bank statements or other proof of funds, where requested.
  • Employment, business, study, invitation, or other supporting documents relevant to the trip.
  • Travel insurance or additional forms, if required for the destination and visa type.

Photo dimensions and supporting documents are destination-specific. Check the current embassy, consulate, or official visa-centre checklist. No visa outcome is guaranteed.
